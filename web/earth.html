🚁 EASA Drone Regulations & Airspace Guide (Netherlands & EU)
🟢 Category A1 (<250g / C0/C1)

Flight over uninvolved people permitted (never over crowds). Max 120m AGL, direct visual line of sight (VLOS). Operator registration ID required when equipped with a camera.

🟡 Category A2 (<2kg / C2)

Maintain at least 30m distance from uninvolved people (5m in low-speed mode). A1/A3 + A2 theoretical certification required.

🔴 Category A3 (<25kg / C3/C4)

Maintain at least 150m distance from residential, commercial, industrial and recreational areas. Safe open buffer field required.

📋 Key Regulatory Rules & Airspace Obligations:
  • Operator Registration: Register as a drone operator with aviation authorities (e.g. RDW) and visibly display your operator number or QR code on the craft.
  • Controlled Airspace (CTR): Flying in airport CTR zones (Schiphol, Rotterdam, Eindhoven, Maastricht, etc.) is strictly forbidden for recreational Open Category drones.
  • Natura 2000 & Wildlife Reserves: Environmental silence zones, bird habitats, and nature reserves are designated no-fly zones.
  • Official Verification: Always check the official GoDrone Map (LVNL) ↗ and aviation inspectorate regulations prior to launch.
